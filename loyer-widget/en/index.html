How much rent can you charge for your apartment or house?

Letting a property in Luxembourg? Estimate your rent for free in four steps, based on public data. A tool by ImmoBuild Kohn in Dudelange.

How the rent is calculated

  • Apartments: rents of signed leases published by the Observatoire de l’Habitat (report no. 24, “cadastre des loyers”), by canton and size, updated with the advertised rent index (2026 T2).
  • Houses: house rental listings collected from Luxembourg property portals (collected on 17 November 2026). The level of signed leases is estimated at about 15% below listings (assumption).
  • Then condition, energy class, outdoor space, floor and parking adjust the rent. You get a range from the conservative rent (signed leases) to the asking rent. Depending on the actual property, the difference can reach 15 to 20%.

The legal cap

In Luxembourg, the annual rent may not exceed 5% of the capital invested in the property. A furnished property may have a separate furniture supplement (at most 1.5% per month of the value of furniture less than 10 years old). Jason will check this with you. Read the law (legilux)

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The estimate is free and commits you to nothing.

Why does the tool ask for my contact details?

Because Jason gets in touch to discuss the result with you. Your details are used for that only. No newsletter.

Is the rent guaranteed?

No. It is an indicative estimate based on public statistics. It does not replace a visit.

Are apartments in my municipality taken into account?

The calculation starts from rents in your canton, then adapts to the size and features of the property. There are too few public rents per municipality for a reliable calculation.
